# Primary-source checks — 5 September 2026

Scope below means the passages inspected, not a claim to have read each source
in full. Source claims are bound to citation IDs in claims.yaml.

## rosenthal1973

Primary scan: https://ranger.uta.edu/~weems/NOTES6319/PAPERSONE/rosenthal1973.pdf

Read all three pages, 65–67: resource costs, strategy sets, pure-equilibrium
construction and example. Congestion includes the selecting player. The proof
guarantees a pure equilibrium in the specified game class, not a global optimum
for all such games. The manuscript derives its own linear-load move identity.

## azar1999

Author-hosted paper: https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~karlin/papers/AzarBKU99.pdf

Read abstract and introduction, Theorems 1.1/1.2 and the process descriptions
through the opening definitions (printed pages 180–183). The paper includes
both sequential allocation and an infinite removal/replacement process. It
samples choices with replacement; the new audit's permutations sample without
replacement. No claim that the present finite run reproduces an asymptotic
maximum-load theorem. Publication/DOI verified in the primary paper.

## zhang2025

Publisher full text: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/10597123241269740

Checked abstract, distributed-cell sorting and damaged-element descriptions,
and publisher issue metadata. The final issue is February 2025, volume 33(1),
25–54; the online-first date is 19 August 2024. The manuscript uses the work as
motivation only and does not attribute the word faultization to these authors.
Their sorting findings are not imported as evidence about this allocation model.

## holm1979

https://www.jstor.org/stable/4615733
Primary scan used in this revision series:
https://www.ime.usp.br/~abe/lista/pdf4R8xPVzCnX.pdf

The earlier GPT-paper source check in this session read the abstract, introduction
and Scheme 1 on printed pages 65–67. That check is reused here; attempts to reopen
the scan for this paper returned an internal error. The relevant result is the
ordered step-down familywise test procedure. The implementation has a known-value
regression test. No simultaneous-interval or equivalence claim is made.

## levin2026
