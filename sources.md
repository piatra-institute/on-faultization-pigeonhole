# Sources

The bibliography is [paper/references.json](paper/references.json), used by
Pandoc's citation processor. The manuscript cites five primary sources:

- Rosenthal (1973): congestion costs include the arriving player; the present
  linear identical-resource argument is a special case, not a theorem about
  every congestion game's global optimum.
- Azar, Broder, Karlin and Upfal (1999): balanced allocation includes sequential
  and removal/replacement processes. Their assumptions and asymptotic endpoints
  differ from the rejecting finite model here.
- Zhang, Goldstein and Levin (2025): distributed sorting under damaged elements;
  motivation, not evidence of cognition in this allocation model. The final
  journal issue is 2025; online publication was in 2024.
- Holm (1979): step-down familywise multiple testing, not simultaneous confidence
  intervals or proof of equivalence for degenerate samples.
- Levin (2026): an explicitly philosophical proposal about mathematical patterns;
  no distinctive transition-level prediction is tested here.

See [source-checks.md](source-checks.md) for URLs, inspected scopes and caveats.
The bibliography is deliberately limited to sources used in the argument.
